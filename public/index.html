04 / THE FINE PRINT

Know the edges.

Does the first creator sale pay 5%?

It can pay the prior rate. Uniswap v4 calculates fees before settlement. The elevated rate applies to subsequent sells in the $HOOK pool.

What starts the window?

A nonzero outgoing transfer from the registered creator wallet, including gifts and liquidity deposits. The rule cannot identify unrelated wallets, and an intermediary can wait until the window expires.

Can I launch from this preview?

Launches and wallet submission are locked. This public preview shows saved evidence and does not run live simulations.
