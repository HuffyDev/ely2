How rules work

What counts as a creator sell?

A direct sale funded by the creator’s wallet through the $HOOK router starts or extends the window. Sales through other routers, intermediary wallets or other pools can bypass the trigger.

What does the execution report cover?

When Foundry is configured, the report runs the draft through a v4 execution fixture. It checks a creator sale and holder buy/sell round trips. The fixture liquidity and trade sizes bound the result.

What can I program?

Set buy and sell LP fees up to 5%, an optional opening window and an optional creator-sell surcharge. Fees use the highest applicable rate.
