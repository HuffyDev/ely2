How rules work

What starts the fee window?

A successful nonzero transfer to another address from the registered creator wallet starts or extends the window, including gifts, liquidity deposits and settlement of sales. v4 calculates fees before settlement, so an initiating sale can pay the prior rate. The elevated rate applies only to subsequent sells in the $HOOK pool. Unrelated wallets cannot be linked to the creator.

What this does not do

It cannot identify unrelated wallets. An intermediary can wait for the window to expire before selling. Gifts and liquidity deposits from the registered wallet also start the window.

What does the execution report cover?

The saved report records a creator sale and ten holder buy/sell round trips on Elysium fork block 3,122,111. Those outcomes apply to the tested liquidity, wallet sizes and fee phases.

What can I program?

Set buy and sell LP fees up to 5%, an optional opening window and an optional sell surcharge after a registered-wallet transfer. Fees use the highest applicable rate.
