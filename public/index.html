How rules work

What starts the fee window?

A successful nonzero transfer to another address from the registered creator wallet starts or extends the window, including gifts, liquidity deposits and settlement of sales. v4 calculates fees before settlement, so an initiating sale can pay the prior rate. The elevated rate applies to subsequent swaps in the $HOOK pool. Unrelated wallets cannot be linked to the creator.

What does the execution report cover?

When Foundry is configured, the report rehearses the supplied launch inputs or uses demo inputs if no wallet is entered. It checks a creator sale and holder buy/sell round trips. The tested liquidity and trade sizes bound the result.

What can I program?

Set buy and sell LP fees up to 5%, an optional opening window and an optional sell surcharge after a registered-wallet transfer. Fees use the highest applicable rate.
